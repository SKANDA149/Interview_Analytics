CONFIRMED: negative raw cosine similarities at severity 0.1 and 0.5 are genuine
(not a computation failure). evaluate_technical_answer() applies
max(0.0, similarity) before scaling to 0-100, so these floor to exactly 0.0.
This means role_swap can push the candidate-attributed transcript's semantic
content far enough from the reference answer (by mixing in interviewer
question text) that similarity crosses zero -- an abrupt, threshold-like
collapse rather than smooth decay. missed_speech never shows this: its
semantic score decays smoothly (5.73 -> 5.73 -> 5.73 -> 4.34) since it only
removes candidate segments rather than contaminating them with interviewer
content.
