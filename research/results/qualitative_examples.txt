Observation: the filler at 53.04s and 117.06s disappear entirely from the
corrupted run's candidate-attributed speech -- the diarization segments
covering those timestamps were flipped from CANDIDATE to INTERVIEWER by
role_swap, so the words are still transcribed (nothing is deleted from the
underlying transcript) but are no longer counted as candidate fillers. This
concretely illustrates the mechanism behind the filler-count distortion
reported in the dose-response tables: role_swap does not remove speech, it
misattributes it, silently erasing real behavioral signal from whichever
speaker's segments get flipped away.
